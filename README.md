## 📊 Featured projects

### [AdventureWorks Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/iamnawaf/adventureworks-sales-dashboard)
A four-page Power BI dashboard (Overview, Map, Products, Customers) for a bike retailer, built on a star-schema model with DAX measures, Top N analysis and synced slicers.
> Top 10 models drive about 90% of revenue, and customers average only 1.44 orders, a clear retention opportunity.

`Power BI` `DAX` `Power Query` `Data Modeling`

### [HR Attrition & Overtime Dashboard](https://github.com/iamnawaf/hr-attrition-overtime-dashboard)
An Arabic-interface Power BI dashboard showing how overtime affects employee attrition across job satisfaction, work-life balance and job level.
> Employees who work overtime leave at nearly 3× the rate of those who don't (30.5% vs 10.4%).

`Power BI` `DAX` `PBIP / TMDL` `HR Analytics`

`Python` `scikit-learn` `Gradio` `Jupyter`

### Amazon Product & Sales Analysis

`Excel` `Power Query (M)` `Reporting`
